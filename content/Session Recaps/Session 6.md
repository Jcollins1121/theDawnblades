# Session 6: 

Upon arriving in Dernhallow, we found the village eerily silent and abandoned. A foul odor hung in the air, while the persistent drone of flies and the sound of wet feeding echoed through the streets. Investigating the disturbance, Belokan attempted to scout ahead but inadvertently alerted the creatures to our presence, drawing us into combat.

Initially facing a lone [[Corpsenasher]], the party was surprisingly confident despite only hearing rumors of these creatures. It was a small quadrupedal creature, boasting a face with only a mouth and dozens of sharp teeth, seemingly easy enough for the 5 of us to handle. However, that confidence quickly faded as its cries summoned roughly eight more of the creatures from throughout the village. Though the battle threatened to overwhelm us, the tide turned after a masterful combination of spells. Jed immobilized a large group with [[Grease]], allowing [[Belokan]] and [[Finn Harrow|Finn]] to unleash coordinated [[Thunderwave]] spells that devastated the clustered corpsenashers.

After defeating the corpsenashers, we ventured further into the city to gather more information and look for potential leads on Silas' cart. In doing so, we quickly came upon Silas' cart with its driver still in the cart, dead, seemingly asphyxiated from the disease along with the rest of the village. His death appeared to be recent, in our estimation he had been dead for 1-2 days. 






After defeating the creatures, we located Silas' cart deeper within the village, only to discover it was a mimic in disguise. We subdued the mimic, recovered the shipment, and returned the goods to Silas in Sablebrook. The contract concluded with a heated dispute, during which Finn assaulted Silas and was briefly detained by the town guard before Leonidas released him with a warning.
