# Session 6: 
Upon arriving in Dernhallow, we found the village eerily silent and abandoned. A foul odor hung in the air, while the persistent drone of flies and the sound of wet feeding echoed through the streets. Investigating the disturbance, Belokan attempted to scout ahead but inadvertently alerted the creatures to our presence, drawing us into combat.

Initially facing a lone [[Corpsenasher]], the party was surprisingly confident despite only hearing rumors of these creatures. It was a small quadrupedal creature, boasting a face with only a mouth and dozens of sharp teeth, seemingly easy enough for the 5 of us to handle. However, that confidence quickly faded as its cries summoned roughly eight more of the creatures from throughout the village. Though the battle threatened to overwhelm us, the tide turned after a masterful combination of spells. Jed immobilized a large group with [Grease](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:grease), allowing [[Belokan]] and [[Finn Harrow|Finn]] to unleash coordinated [Thunderwave](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:thunderwave) spells that devastated the clustered corpsenashers.

After defeating the corpsenashers, we ventured further into the city to gather more information and look for potential leads on Silas' cart. In doing so, we quickly came upon Silas' cart with its driver still in the cart, dead, seemingly asphyxiated from the disease along with the rest of the village. His death appeared to be recent, in our estimation he had been dead for no longer than 1-2 days. While we searched the surrounding area for additional leads, [[Finn Harrow|Finn]] investigated the back of the carriage and discovered the cargo we had been sent to recover. As he reached out with his trident to inspect the chest, it suddenly attacked. The cargo was a mimic in disguise. We engaged the creature and, after a brief struggle, managed to subdue it without killing it so that we could recover the gold bounty.

![[Session6.png|342]]

Exhausted from the day's events and with tempers running high, we returned to Sablebrook. [[Lassaran]] was the first to find Silas and promptly dragged him outside the tavern so we could confront him and collect our payment. We questioned him about the contents of his shipment, but he insisted he had neither loaded the cart himself nor knew what had been placed inside it. Showing little remorse, he tossed the bag of gold onto the ground at our feet and declared that the contract had been fulfilled.

[[Finn Harrow|Finn]] took particular exception to Silas' indifference. Seizing him by the collar, Finn threw him to the ground and rubbed his face into the dirt. The commotion quickly drew the attention of a town guard, who escorted both men away for questioning. After hearing both sides of the story, Leonidas chose to show Finn some leniency, acknowledging that we had already uncovered and reported the mysterious tunnel system, proving our good intentions despite the altercation. Meanwhile, back at the tavern, the subdued mimic awoke from its slumber. With [[Finn Harrow|Finn]] and [[Jericho Hawthorne|Jericho]] absent, [[Lassaran]], [[Belokan]], and [[Jed Tokki|Jed]] swiftly dispatched the creature before it could pose any further threat.
