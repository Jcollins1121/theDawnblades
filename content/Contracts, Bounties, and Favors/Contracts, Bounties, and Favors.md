# Contracts, Bounties, and Favors 
![[Torrick_Bounty.png|444]]

### Ongoing: 

- #### WANTED!!! Torrick the Red
	By order of The Crown, let it be known that Torrick the Red is hereby declared an outlaw and enemy of all civilized lands. He stands accused of the razing of Barrow's End and the murder of every living soul within it. 
	
	A reward of 250 gold pieces will be paid for his capture or confirmed death. 
	
	Torrick is considered armed, ruthless, and exceedingly dangerous. He is known to travel  with several associates. Any who offer him shelter, aid, or warning shall be judged accomplices and punished accordingly. 
	
	Let all Magistrates, soldiers, mercenaries, and loyal subjects take heed: Bring him to  justice, dead or alive.

- ####  **Recover the Missing Children**
    Mayor Edric Van Holt is offering 60 gold for the retrieval of of the remains of the two infants (Adam Moonsong, age 2 weeks, and Eleanor Windrider, age 6 months). that went missing over the past month, so that they may be put to final rest. And additional 20 gold is offered for information on the creature responsible, with an additional 30 on top of that for its head. Report to town hall to claim the contract.

- #### **Corpse Theft**
	The Mayor is offering a 50 gold reward for the capture of whomever is resposible for the theft of two corpses from Sablebrook Cemetary. The first corpse was Tiffany Chandler, a teenage girl who dronwned in the Redbrook 3 weeks ago. The second belonged to Dragar Stonebeard, the local blacksmith, who passed away in his sleep 9 days ago. Cause of death was recorded as old age by coroner Milo Fairweather. both corpses were dug up even 48 hours after internment. The local guard has been working the case but currently are reporting no leads. If necromancy is the culprit, Mayor Van Holt is offering an additional 15 gold per reanimated put to rest and an additional 100 for bringing in the necromancer.

- #### **Stolen Silver** 
	Madame Rosette, owner of The Velvet Cup, is offering 25g for the return of a silver tea tea set. It was stolen from the storeroom late night Aprilis 15th. A witness saw “a small, shadowy figure” sneaking into the alley behind the shop. The Guard has yet to investigate, stating they are too currnely too busy with other matters.

His cart was stolen by bandits, goods still stowed, shortly after he left [[Sablebrook]], on the road to [[Vaeloria]]. Silas can be found at the Scampering Stag. 
	 
	***Status***: Completed 
	***Gold*** ***Gained***: 150gp 
	***Notes***: Pursued a band of highway thieves after witnessing them ambush another caravan. Captured one bandit, tracked the others to their hideout, and uncovered a lead pointing toward [[Dernhallow]]. Recovered Silas' cart from the abandoned village after defeating a pack of Corpenashers and subduing a mimic disguised as the cargo. Returned the shipment safely, though the job ended with [[Finn Harrow|Finn]] being questioned by the town guard following a dispute with Silas.
