### Overview: 
Jericho Hawthorne is an Eladrin warlock, artisan, and former member of the [[Argent Exchange Guild]]. Serious, curious, and fiercely self-reliant, he joined the [[Dawnblades]] after being entrusted with an unfinished route by the mysterious entity known as [[The Still Whisper]]. Though polite and charismatic, Jericho tends to keep others at a careful distance. He is often found asking questions, studying unfamiliar objects, or carving strange wooden figures during quiet moments.
